GarageResale

Privacy notice

Updated 12 October 2026

About GarageResale

GarageResale is a personal resale tool used by a seller to manage their inventory, marketplace listings, sales and buyer conversations. This notice describes its eBay integration in a local installation operated on the seller’s computer.

Information used

When the seller authorizes eBay access, GarageResale can process the seller’s account identity, listing identifiers and content, product photos, prices, order identifiers, buyer usernames, sale amounts, and relevant buyer and seller messages and accessible message attachments. It also retrieves public listing information for product price research. It does not collect eBay passwords or process payment-card details.

Why information is used

Information is used to synchronize listings created through GarageResale, display related sales conversations, record sales, research comparable asking prices and prepare seller-reviewed listing content and replies. Publishing and message sending depend on the seller’s enabled controls. GarageResale is not used to sell buyer information or create advertising profiles.

Storage and services

In the local setup, saved inventory, sales and messages are stored in the seller’s local database; photos and attachments use the seller’s configured local or private object storage. Account credentials are retained privately by the installation. This public notice does not provide access to the application, credentials, inventory or buyer messages.

GarageResale exchanges information with eBay to perform the authorized operations. If the seller enables AI features, selected product information, photos or conversation context may be sent to the configured AI provider to generate identification, listing content or reply drafts. The AI provider’s terms and privacy practices apply to that processing. The seller controls whether AI features are enabled.

Retention and account access

Records, photos and messages remain until the seller removes them from the relevant database, storage and backups. Removing an item from the interface may retain historical records or stored files. Disconnecting the integration or revoking authorization through eBay stops authorized account access; it does not automatically remove information already saved in the installation or backups.

Questions and requests

For questions about information associated with a sale, or requests to review, correct or remove that information, contact the seller through the eBay conversation associated with the listing. The seller manages the local records and any backups.

Changes

The seller should update this notice when the installation’s data use, hosting or enabled services change.
